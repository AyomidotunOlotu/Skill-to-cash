Pick Your Skill

Each skill has different entry barriers and earning timelines. Pick the one that matches your current tools, personality, and available time. Pick one. Commit fully.

🎨
Graphic Design
Flyers, logos, social media graphics, posters, business cards. Massive demand from small businesses, churches, events, student unions, and politicians year-round.
₦5k–₦50k per job
🎬
Video Editing
Edit raw footage, add music, subtitles, transitions. Businesses making reels, YouTube creators, event coverage, product promo videos. Very high demand right now.
₦10k–₦80k per video
✍️
Copywriting
Write captions, ads, product descriptions, email newsletters that make people buy. Pure skill — no tools needed beyond a phone. Works on mobile entirely.
₦5k–₦60k per project
📱
Social Media Management
Run a business's Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok page. Create content, post consistently, engage followers. Monthly retainer model = recurring income every month.
₦20k–₦80k/month
🌐
Web Design (WordPress)
Build websites for small businesses. WordPress powers 43% of the internet. Nigerians are spending ₦50k–₦200k on websites and getting scammed. You can do it better.
₦50k–₦200k per site
Ask yourself: Do I have a laptop? → Web design, video editing, or graphic design. Only a phone? → Copywriting or social media management. Do I like writing? → Copywriting. Hate writing? → Design or video. Pick one. Start today.
Graphic Design has the fastest pipeline — churches, student groups, shops, event organizers. With 2 weeks of practice you can charge for flyers. Copywriting is a close second because once you can write a converting caption, pitching is immediate. Social media management takes slightly longer because clients want to see proof before trusting you with their page.
Perfect. Everyone starts at zero. The resources in Section 02 are specifically for beginners. You don't need any prior knowledge — just 1–2 hours daily. Graphic Design via Canva has the gentlest learning curve. You can be producing decent client-ready work in under 7 days.
