Learn It For Free

Every resource below is free or has a completely usable free tier. No paid course is required to get your first client. The rule: learn a concept → immediately apply it to a practice project → repeat. Stop watching tutorials without doing the work alongside them.
🎨 Graphic Design
Resource Where Type What You Learn
Canva Design School canva.com/learn FREE TOOL Built-in beginner curriculum — layouts, color, typography. Start here Day 1.
Gareth David Studio YouTube YOUTUBE Search "Canva tutorial for beginners 2024." Best channel for Canva + Photoshop. Very clear.
Satori Graphics YouTube YOUTUBE Design principles (typography, color theory, hierarchy). Watch after Canva basics — levels you up.
Piximperfect YouTube YOUTUBE Photoshop from absolute zero. "Learn Photoshop in 30 Days" playlist is the best free course online.
The Futur (Chris Do) YouTube YOUTUBE How to price design work, handle clients, and think like a design business owner. Watch this once you're earning.
Canva (Tool) canva.com FREE TOOL Your primary tool. Free plan is enough to earn your first ₦100k. Pro is optional later.
Figma (Tool) figma.com FREE TOOL More professional UI/social media design. Free tier is very generous. Worth learning after Canva.
Freepik / Pexels / Unsplash freepik.com · pexels.com FREE Free stock images, vectors, and templates for your designs. Use these in client work.
Fontpair + Google Fonts fontpair.co · fonts.google.com FREE Choose good font combinations fast. Typography is 70% of what makes a design look professional.
🎯 PATH: Canva (Day 1–3) → Gareth David Studio YouTube (Day 4–10) → Satori Graphics for theory → Piximperfect when you move to Photoshop. In 2 weeks you'll be able to charge.
🎬 Video Editing
Resource Where Type What You Learn
CapCut (Mobile) App Store / Play Store FREE TOOL Best free mobile editor. Reels, subtitles, transitions, templates. Start here if you only have a phone.
Zhane Creative YouTube YOUTUBE Search "CapCut tutorial for beginners." Best CapCut channel — covers reels, trending edits, captions.
VN Video Editor (Mobile) App Store / Play Store FREE TOOL More advanced than CapCut — better timeline control, good for longer YouTube-style videos.
DaVinci Resolve (PC) blackmagicdesign.com FREE TOOL Industry-standard, completely free. Hollywood editors use this. Learn it if you have a decent laptop.
Casey Faris YouTube YOUTUBE Best beginner-to-intermediate DaVinci Resolve course on YouTube. "DaVinci Resolve for Beginners" playlist.
Justin Odisho YouTube YOUTUBE Adobe Premiere Pro tutorials — transitions, colour grading, motion graphics. Great for levelling up.
Mango Street YouTube YOUTUBE Storytelling through editing. Teaches you to think like a visual director, not just a button-presser.
Mixkit / Pixabay mixkit.co · pixabay.com FREE Free stock footage, sound effects, and music for client videos. Avoids copyright issues.
🎯 PATH: CapCut (phone users, Day 1–3) OR DaVinci Resolve (laptop users) → Casey Faris or Zhane Creative → Mango Street for storytelling. Chargeable in 3–4 weeks.
✍️ Copywriting
Resource Where Type What You Learn
Alex Cattoni YouTube YOUTUBE Modern copywriting from scratch — captions, email, sales pages. "How to become a copywriter" series is the best free starting point.
Marketing Examples (Harry Dry) marketingexamples.com FREE Real-world breakdowns of what makes copy work. Read 1 case study per day. Changes how you see writing instantly.
The Boron Letters (Gary Halbert) Search "Boron Letters free PDF" FREE Classic copywriting fundamentals written as letters to a son. Old but foundational. Read it once.
Copyhackers Blog copyhackers.com FREE Deep-dive articles on conversion copy. Read 1 article daily. Focus on the "Copywriting 101" category first.
Nnamdi Azodo Twitter / X FREE Nigerian copywriter. Context-relevant threads and examples. Follow and read his older posts — huge value.
Swiped.co swiped.co FREE A swipe file of real ads and copy that converted. Study structure, not just words. Your "textbook" library.
Copy.ai / Claude copy.ai · claude.ai FREE TOOL Use AI to generate first drafts, then rewrite them in your voice. Speeds up learning and delivery. Do NOT just submit AI output raw.
🎯 PATH: Alex Cattoni YouTube playlist (Week 1) → Boron Letters PDF (Week 1–2) → Marketing Examples daily (ongoing) → Build a swipe file of good Nigerian ads. Pitch in Week 2.

🌐 Web Design (WordPress)
Resource Where Type What You Learn
WPBeginner YouTube + wpbeginner.com YOUTUBE FREE The most comprehensive free WordPress resource online. Start with "WordPress for Beginners" full course on YouTube.
Darrel Wilson YouTube YOUTUBE Build full business websites step-by-step with Elementor. Very practical, Nigerian context works perfectly.
Ferdy Korpershoek YouTube YOUTUBE Extremely detailed WordPress tutorials. Best for building real client sites — covers e-commerce, portfolios, landing pages.
Elementor Academy elementor.com/academy FREE Official free training for Elementor (most popular drag-and-drop WordPress builder). Covers page design and advanced features.
WordPress.tv wordpress.tv FREE Official WordPress tutorials and conference talks. Use as supplementary material alongside YouTube.
Local by Flywheel (Tool) localwp.com FREE TOOL Build WordPress sites offline on your laptop — no hosting needed to practice. Free, works without internet.
Astra Theme + Starter Templates wpastra.com FREE Free professional WordPress theme + 200+ one-click template sites. Makes your first site look high-quality immediately.
Hostinger Nigeria hostinger.com.ng PAID Affordable hosting (~₦4k–₦8k/year). Recommend to clients or use for your own portfolio site. Reliable in Nigeria.
🎯 PATH: Local by Flywheel install (Day 1) → WPBeginner full YouTube course (Week 1–2) → Darrel Wilson or Ferdy (Week 2–3 practice sites) → Build 2 dummy sites → Pitch. Requires a laptop.
📦 Campus Gadget Reselling
Resource Where Type How to Use It
Jiji.ng jiji.ng FREE Research current market prices for used and new gadgets in Nigeria. Know what to buy low and sell at a fair margin.
Jumia / Konga jumia.com.ng FREE Source new accessories (earphones, cables, cases, power banks) at competitive prices. Compare multiple sellers before buying.
Computer Village, Ikeja (Lagos) / Wuse Market (Abuja) Physical Market FREE Buy accessories and refurbished gadgets at wholesale prices. Build a relationship with 1–2 trusted vendors for consistent supply.
WhatsApp Business (Tool) App Store / Play Store FREE TOOL Set up a business profile with your product catalog. Post daily status updates. Automate quick replies. Your free storefront.
Canva (Product Flyers) canva.com FREE TOOL Create product promo graphics for WhatsApp Status. A professional-looking flyer makes items sell faster at better prices.
Alibaba / 1688 Research alibaba.com FREE Research factory prices for accessories. Not for buying directly as a student (MOQs are high), but helps you understand margins.
🎯 PATH: Set up WhatsApp Business (Day 1) → Research prices on Jiji (Day 1–2) → Source first batch of accessories (₦5k–₦10k) → Post on WhatsApp Status daily → Focus on accessories with postgrads and staff who spend more.
Don't download 10 resources and use none. Pick the ONE starting resource for your skill, go all the way through it, apply every concept live. You'll learn more in 2 focused weeks than 2 months of passive watching.
